Spanish to Ukrainian for Kobo

Kobo dicthtml archive. Copy it to your Kobo's .kobo/custom-dict/ folder, then restart the device.

Download dicthtml-es-uk.zip
  1. Connect your Kobo to your computer by USB.
  2. Copy the downloaded ZIP into .kobo/custom-dict/.
  3. Eject and restart your Kobo.
  4. Open a Spanish book and tap a word to look it up.

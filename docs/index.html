Setup in under a minute

Install on your Kobo

  1. Download the dictionary you want (dicthtml-es-uk.zip or dicthtml-en-uk.zip).
  2. Connect your Kobo to your computer with a USB cable.
  3. Copy the ZIP into the .kobo/custom-dict/ folder on the device (create it if it isn't there).
  4. Safely eject your Kobo and restart it.
  5. Open a Spanish or English book, long-press any word, and pick the matching dictionary (Español → українська or English → українська).

Works on Kobo e-readers that support custom dictionaries — Clara, Libra, Sage, Elipsa, Forma, Aura, Glo, and Nia series. Not for Kindle (different format — ask below if you'd like one).

Good to know

FAQ

Is this an official or hand-edited dictionary?
No. It's an independent project built from open Wiktionary data, with translations generated and reviewed for consistency. It's comprehensive and genuinely useful for reading, but it's a community-grade resource, not a hand-edited academic dictionary. Spot an error? Open an issue and I'll fix it.
Will it slow down my Kobo?
No — each dictionary is small and loads instantly.
How much does it cost?
Both dictionaries are free. Donations are welcome, and custom builds for other devices or language pairs are done for a small donation.
